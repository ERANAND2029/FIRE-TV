# Family Watch Referee - 2 Min 30 Sec Hackathon Demo Script

**Track:** Amazon Developer Hackathon - Fire TV Track  
**Platform:** Fire OS / Android TV (Leanback 1080p, 10-foot UI)  
**Total Time:** 02:30  

---

### [00:00 - 00:25] The Problem & The Solution
- **Visual:** Clean 1080p Android TV / Fire TV screen showing **Family Watch Referee** with deep cinematic slate theme, ambient glow, and clock header.
- **Narrator (English/Hinglish):**
  > "Every evening in millions of Indian households, the exact same fight happens: *'Aaj kya dekhein?'* Papa wants a grounded thriller, Mummy wants lighthearted comedy, Bhai wants fast-paced sci-fi, and little Chhotu can't watch anything violent. 
  > Half an hour gets wasted just scrolling through OTT menus. 
  > This is **Family Watch Referee**—the world's first AI-powered household compromise engine built specifically for Fire TV and the living room 10-foot experience."

---

### [00:25 - 00:55] Profile Selection & The "Chhotu" Kid-Safety Guarantee
- **Action on Remote:** Use Left/Right D-Pad arrows to move focus between the four profile cards (**Papa**, **Mummy**, **Bhai**, **Chhotu**). Notice the thick gold focus ring, 1.08x smooth zoom, and audio chimes.
- **Action on Remote:** Press **OK / Enter** on Bhai to toggle him to 'Skipping' (saying he's out studying tonight), then press **OK** again to bring him back in.
- **Narrator:**
  > "Using just the Fire TV remote D-pad, anyone sitting on the sofa can join or skip tonight's watch party with a single click. 
  > Notice Chhotu's card: when Chhotu is active, our AI referee activates a strict *Kid-Safety Lock*. Any title not certified 'U' is immediately eliminated from the decision pool, guaranteeing 100% peace of mind for parents."

---

### [00:55 - 01:25] Per-Profile Mood & Runtime Constraints
- **Action on Remote:** Focus on **Papa** -> Press D-Pad Down into the preference panel.
- **Action on Remote:** Change Papa's Mood to **Family** and Max Runtime to **90 min**.
- **Action on Remote:** Navigate back up and over to **Mummy** -> Press Down, set Mood to **Comedy** and Max Runtime to **90 min**.
- **Action on Remote:** Navigate to **Bhai** -> set Mood to **Action**, Max Runtime to **120 min**.
- **Narrator:**
  > "Each member has their own mood and bedtime tolerance. Papa has early morning meetings tomorrow, so he caps runtime at 90 minutes. 
  > Our arbitration engine treats runtime as a hard mathematical threshold—any title exceeding the lowest bedtime limit suffers a steep penalty."

---

### [01:25 - 01:50] The "Decide!" AI Arbitrator in Action
- **Action on Remote:** Press D-Pad Down to the big gold button: **"DECIDE FOR THE HOUSEHOLD!"** -> Press **OK**.
- **Visual:** Screen switches smoothly to the **"Referee is Negotiating..."** thinking card with spinning whistle animation and live status lines (*"Applying strict Chhotu kid-safety boundaries..."*, *"Simulating consensus..."*).
- **Visual:** Winner screen pops up: **"Chai Aur Chandrayaan"** (92 min, U-rated, 98% Match Score).
- **Narrator:**
  > "Boom! The referee negotiates all constraints simultaneously. It picked 'Chai Aur Chandrayaan'—U-rated for Chhotu, 92 minutes to respect Papa's bedtime, with wholesome comedy for Mummy and sci-fi elements for Bhai."

---

### [01:50 - 02:10] Natural Hinglish Explanation & Alternatives
- **Visual:** Focus on the Referee Verdict quote box:
  > *"Chhotu ke liye safe U-rating, Papa ko mast story, aur 92 min mein bedtime sorted!"*
- **Action on Remote:** Press D-Pad Right to **"DECIDE AGAIN (NEXT BEST)"** -> Press **OK**.
- **Visual:** Winner card instantly transitions to the runner-up compromise title (*"Samosa Express 24/7"*), updating the score breakdown and explanation dynamically.
- **Narrator:**
  > "Instead of dry algorithmic scores, the Referee gives the family a friendly, witty Hinglish verdict that everyone understands. 
  > And if someone still wants an alternative, 'Decide Again' instantly surfaces the runner-up compromise without restarting the argument."

---

### [02:10 - 02:30] Simulated Play & Household Memory Adaptation
- **Action on Remote:** Focus on **"PLAY NOW"** -> Press **OK**.
- **Visual:** Simulated Player opens with progress bar moving, title details, and play/pause toggle.
- **Action on Remote:** Press **Back** to return to Home.
- **Visual:** The top **AI Household Memory Ribbon** updates in real-time:
  > *"💡 AI Household Memory: Pichli baar 'Chai Aur Chandrayaan' dekhi thi, aaj household preferences ke hisaab se fresh compromise choose karenge."*
- **Narrator:**
  > "Our persistent household memory remembers what the family watched, avoids repetitive recommendations, and adapts to weekend vs. weekday prime times. 
  > Zero setup, zero arguments, 100% remote-friendly. Family Watch Referee turns movie night from a battle into bonding!"
